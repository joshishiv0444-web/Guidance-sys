== Mobile Banking & General Payments FAQ ==

Q: How do I add a bank account to my UPI app?
A: Open your UPI app, go to "Add Bank Account", select your bank from the list, verify your mobile number, and set a UPI PIN using your debit card details.

Q: What is NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS?
A: These are different bank transfer methods:
- NEFT: National Electronic Funds Transfer — works in batches, usually within 2 hours.
- RTGS: Real-Time Gross Settlement — instant transfer for large amounts (₹2 lakh+).
- IMPS: Immediate Payment Service — instant transfer, available 24/7, any amount.

Q: I entered the wrong account number. Can I cancel the transfer?
A: Unfortunately, once a bank transfer is confirmed it usually cannot be reversed automatically. Contact your bank immediately and file a grievance. They will try to recover the funds.

Q: What is a beneficiary?
A: A beneficiary is the person or account you want to send money to. In online banking you need to add them as a beneficiary before sending large transfers. This takes 30 minutes to 24 hours to activate as a security measure.

Q: What should I do if my transaction failed but money was deducted?
A: In most cases, failed transactions are automatically reversed within 3-5 business days. If not, raise a dispute through the app or call your bank's customer care.

Q: What is a bank account number vs IFSC code?
A: Your account number is unique to YOUR account (typically 9-18 digits). The IFSC code identifies your specific BRANCH. Both are needed together to receive a bank transfer.

Q: Is it safe to share my account number and IFSC code?
A: Yes, it is generally safe to share your account number and IFSC code with people who need to SEND you money. These details alone cannot be used to take money from your account.
