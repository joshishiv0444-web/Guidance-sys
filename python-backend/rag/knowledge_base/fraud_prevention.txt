== Fraud & Scam Prevention Guide ==

Common Types of Digital Fraud:
1. Remote Access Scams: Scammer asks you to install apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer and takes control of your phone.
2. UPI Collect Request Scams: Scammer sends a UPI collect request pretending to "send" you money — approving it will DEDUCT money from your account.
3. KYC Update Scams: Fake messages saying your account will be blocked unless you share your Aadhaar, PAN, or OTP.
4. Lottery or Prize Scams: Messages saying you won a prize and need to pay a small fee to claim it.
5. Fake Customer Care Numbers: Fraudulent numbers appearing in internet searches posing as bank helplines.

High Risk Warning Signs:
- Someone is asking you to install a remote-control app.
- Someone is asking for your OTP, PIN, password, or Aadhaar number.
- You received a UPI "collect" request from an unknown person.
- You are being rushed to make a quick payment.
- A deal sounds too good to be true.

What to do if you suspect fraud?
1. Stop the transaction immediately — do not proceed.
2. Disconnect the call or close the app.
3. Call your bank's official helpline (number on the back of your debit card).
4. Report the fraud at the National Cyber Crime helpline: 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Block your card or account if money was transferred.

Safe Banking Practices:
- Only use the official app downloaded from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
- Never click links in SMS or WhatsApp to access your bank account.
- Regularly check your account statements for unknown transactions.
- Use a strong UPI PIN and change it periodically.
