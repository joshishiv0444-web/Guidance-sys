== UPI Payments Guide ==

What is UPI?
UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a real-time payment system that allows you to transfer money directly between bank accounts using a mobile app. It is safe, fast, and free for most transfers.

What is a UPI ID?
A UPI ID (also called a Virtual Payment Address or VPA) looks like: name@bank (e.g., john@upi, 9876543210@paytm). It is a unique address linked to your bank account.

How to make a UPI payment?
1. Open your UPI-enabled app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, etc.).
2. Tap the "Send Money" or "Pay" option.
3. Enter the recipient's UPI ID in the input field.
4. Enter the amount you want to send.
5. Review the details carefully — check the name that appears after entering the UPI ID.
6. Enter your UPI PIN to authorize the payment.
7. Tap the Pay button.

Important Safety Rules for UPI:
- NEVER share your UPI PIN with anyone, including bank employees.
- If someone asks you to enter your PIN to RECEIVE money — that is a SCAM. Stop immediately.
- Always verify the payee's name that appears on screen before confirming.
- You do NOT need a PIN to receive money — only to send money.

What is a UPI PIN?
Your UPI PIN is a 4 or 6 digit secret number you set when you register your bank account on a UPI app. Keep it private at all times.
